EVIDENCE PLANNING LOOP

Integrate the mapper into the existing EvidencePlanner flow.

The desired behavior is:

1. EvidencePlanner creates the initial evidence plan.

2. Evidence collectors produce evidence.

3. EvidenceDiversityMapperAdapter maps the current EvidenceBundle and obtains conceptual coverage.

4. Coverage gaps are returned to EvidencePlanner.

5. EvidencePlanner decides which gaps justify additional collection.

6. Existing evidence collectors generate or gather the requested additional evidence.

7. New evidence is mapped again.

8. The process stops when:
   required coverage conditions are satisfied;
   a decisive failure exists;
   the evidence budget is exhausted;
   or further collection is not justified.

Do not implement an unbounded loop.

Use the existing evidence budget and stopping semantics where available.

If no appropriate budget exists, add the smallest explicit budget abstraction necessary.

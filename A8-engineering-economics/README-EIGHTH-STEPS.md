# Component 8 — Engineering Productivity & Automation Economics

## 1. The question Component 8 answers

The original L1 automation proposal is economically motivated.

The target work is performed in part through variable-cost offshore contractor
capacity.

The proposed automation hypothesis is therefore not merely:

> AI can write code.

It is closer to:

> AI, supervised by a smaller group of selected staff, can safely deliver a
> meaningful portion of suitable L1 engineering changes with lower unit cost,
> lower active human effort, and acceptable or improved delivery performance.

Component 8 makes that hypothesis measurable.
